Local SEO audit

Audit structure

The areas I look at when I review a local business: profile, website, citations and competitors. This shows the format only. It isn't a real client's result.

Live client projects and verified search results will be added here as I'm able to share them.

I build websites around what a local business does, where it operates, and how people find and contact it.

Every project starts with understanding the business: its services, the places it works in, its competitors and what the owner wants the website to do. I don't run the same checklist on every client, and I don't add pages just to make a site look bigger.

  1. 1

    Research

    I look at what the business really does, where it works, what people search for, and how competitors present the same services.

  2. 2

    Strategy

    I decide what's worth building or fixing first: which pages, which services, which areas, and what the Google Business Profile needs to match.

  3. 3

    Development and Optimization

    I build or improve the site and profile, then check titles, headings, internal links, contact options and the mobile layout.

  4. 4

    Review and Improvement

    Once it's live, I look at the data that's available, like Search Console, and tell you what's working and what to do next.

Questions I want answered before I build or change anything

  • What does the business actually do?
  • Which areas does it genuinely serve?
  • What are people likely to search for?
  • How do competitors present their services?
  • Which pages would be genuinely useful?
  • Does the Google Business Profile describe the business accurately?
  • Do the website and the profile say the same thing?
  • Does the site make it easy to call or enquire?
  • What should be fixed first, based on likely value?
